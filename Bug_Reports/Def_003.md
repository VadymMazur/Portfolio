## bug_001: Footer contact details are not actionable

- **Steps:** open the home page on mobile; scroll to the footer; tap the phone number and clinic location.
- **Reported actual:** contact/location text is difficult to distinguish from links and does not open dialing or map navigation.
- **Proposed expected:** actionable contact details with clear affordances, subject to the product's contact-flow requirements.
- **Impact:** additional effort to contact the clinic. The original title claimed a missing registration form, but these steps do not establish that defect. A consultation popup is a feature proposal, not a proven requirement.
- **Original classification:** S2 Critical / P2 High; requires impact-based reassessment.
